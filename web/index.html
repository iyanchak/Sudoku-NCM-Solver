Self-Solving Sudoku

Next-Concept Modeling style — predicts all cells in parallel, then rewrites its own output level by level. arXiv 2610.14162

Press Generate to create a Sudoku
Play it by hand, or press Solve to watch the AI
Play manually: click a cell and type 1-9 (arrows move, Backspace erases, Esc deselects) or use the pad — small numbers show how many of each digit remain. Red cells clash with a row, column or box.
Or watch the AI: Level 0 predicts every empty cell at once (blue), then each level re-predicts the broken parts in parallel and rewrites earlier output (amber flash) — self-correction that sequential "ChatGPT-style" generation cannot do. Every AI solution is verified against Knuth's Dancing Links exact-cover solver.
